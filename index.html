About Me

Where precision engineering
meets data intelligence

I am a PhD Mechanical Engineer and Gold Medal MSc Data Analyst with over a decade of hands-on experience spanning government-funded research, experimental testing, CAD design, and advanced analytics.

My career began at IGCAR, India's Dept. of Atomic Energy, where I led rigorous experimental programmes on welding emission control — achieving a 70% reduction in carcinogenic Cr(VI) through nano-composite flux innovation, validated to NIOSH and AWS international standards.

As Principal Investigator at Marian Engineering College, I built research labs from scratch, led 30+ person teams, secured government funding, and mentored students into engineers. My EV battery thermoelectric cooling system earned a Best Paper Award and granted patent.

I recently completed an MSc in Data Analytics at DkIT, Ireland — graduating First Class with the President's Gold Medal — and now combine my engineering depth with modern data science tools to deliver both technical and analytical impact.

I currently work as an R&D Engineer at E&I Engineering Limited (Vertiv) in Burnfoot, Co. Donegal, developing power distribution products including busbar tap-off boxes and switchgear — bringing together my experimental research background and CAD/analytics skillset in an industrial R&D setting.
